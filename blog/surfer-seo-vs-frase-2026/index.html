How they actually compare, category by category

01

Methodology

How we tested, and why these scores are honest

We didn't score these from a feature list. We ran the same real jobs through both tools. We took one keyword from research to a published-ready draft. We optimized an existing article to lift its score. We built a brief for a writer and timed how long each path took.

Where a number could be checked, like plan limits, we flag it to confirm on each vendor's site, because content-tool pricing shifts often. Every score below maps back to one of those jobs, not to marketing copy.

And we tried to prove ourselves wrong. The lazy take is that these tools do the same thing. They don't. One is an optimization instrument; the other is a research-and-writing workshop. Once you see that, the pick gets obvious.

02

Round 1 · Optimization

On-page optimization: where Surfer sets the standard

This is Surfer's home turf. The Content Editor reads the live SERP for your keyword and scores your draft in real time on terms, headings, length and structure. You watch the number climb as you write, which takes the guesswork out of hitting a target before you hit publish.

Frase optimizes too, and its score is useful, but the term data is less granular than Surfer's NLP lists. For a final optimization pass that squeezes out every ranking signal, Surfer is simply sharper.

So if your month is optimizing drafts to match what already ranks, this one category can justify the subscription, and it leans Surfer.

Who wins this round

Surfer SEO

The most precise, SERP-driven on-page optimization.

03

Round 2 · Research

Research and briefs: Frase gets you moving faster

Frase starts a step earlier. Give it a keyword and it pulls the SERP, summarizes the ranking pages and assembles a brief and outline in seconds. For planning a piece or handing a writer a clear brief, it is genuinely quick.

Surfer has SERP analysis too, but Frase's research-to-brief flow is tighter and faster to act on. If the slow part of your week is turning a keyword into a plan, Frase removes most of that friction.

Neither leaves you short of data. The real choice is Surfer's optimization depth versus Frase's speed from keyword to brief.

“

Frase turns a blank page into a plan before you've finished your coffee.

Who wins this round

Frase

Fastest keyword-to-brief research flow.

04

Round 3 · AI writing

AI drafting: core for Frase, a helper for Surfer

Frase treats writing as a first-class job. Its AI drafts outlines, intros and full sections, so you can stay in one tool from keyword to first draft. For solo writers and small teams shipping volume, that single flow is the draw.

Surfer has an AI writer and a Jasper integration, but drafting is layered on top of optimization rather than the center of the product. It's capable, just not the reason you buy Surfer.

If AI drafting is central to how you work, weight this heavily, and it points at Frase.

Who wins this round

Frase

AI writing is the core, so drafting is faster end to end.

05

Round 4 · Price

The real cost (not just the sticker price)

Read the chart carefully, and confirm both on their sites, because these plans change. Frase's Solo plan is the cheapest way in by a wide margin, which makes it an easy yes for a solo writer. Surfer's entry plan costs more and the lower tiers cap how many articles you can optimize.

But cheaper depends on configuration. Frase's strongest AI and optimization features sit behind a paid Pro add-on, so a like-for-like setup narrows the gap. And Surfer's price buys depth, auditing and integrations that a volume team will actually use.

Simple rule: price the setup you'll really run, add-ons included. A solo writer drafting from scratch → Frase is cheaper in practice. An agency optimizing at volume → Surfer earns its higher number.

Surfer SEO

Best for: SEO teams and agencies who want precise, SERP-driven on-page optimization and auditing.

Surfer is built around one idea done very well: take what already ranks and tell you exactly how to match it. The Content Editor scores your draft live against the top SERP results on terms, headings, length and structure. For hitting a target before you publish, nothing else is this precise.

Beyond the editor you get a SERP analyzer, a site audit, and solid integrations into Google Docs, WordPress and even Jasper. For an agency pushing volume with consistent quality, that pipeline is the whole point.

The trade-offs are price and focus. Entry plans cost more than Frase and cap how many articles you can optimize, and the AI writer is a helper, not the star. If your job is optimization precision, that focus is a feature.

FAQ

Is Surfer or Frase better for SEO content? +

For on-page optimization precision, Surfer. Its Content Editor scores drafts against the live SERP in more granular detail. For research, briefs and AI drafting in one place, Frase is the smoother, cheaper flow.

Which is better for AI writing? +

Frase. AI drafting is a core feature, so going from keyword to first draft is faster. Surfer's AI writer is a helper layered on top of its optimization tools.

Which is cheaper? +

Frase. Its Solo plan is a few dollars a month, far below Surfer's entry plan. Note Frase's strongest AI and optimization features need the paid Pro add-on, so compare the real configuration you'd use.

Do I need both Surfer and Frase? +

Most people don't. Some teams research and draft in Frase, then do a final optimization pass in Surfer, but that's a budget choice, not a requirement.

Do they integrate with Google Docs or WordPress? +

Surfer integrates with Google Docs and WordPress and offers a Jasper integration. Frase focuses on its own editor. Confirm current integrations on each site.

Can either replace a full SEO suite like Semrush or Ahrefs? +

No. Surfer and Frase are content optimization and writing tools, not backlink or rank-tracking suites. Many teams pair one of them with Semrush or Ahrefs.

The verdict

Be straight about the scores first: Frase wins more of the eight categories, mostly on research, AI drafting, ease and price. Surfer pulls ahead on the average because its optimization and NLP-term depth are so far in front. Neither wins outright, it depends on your workflow.

If you want the one-line default for teams whose job is ranking precision, optimizing drafts to match the SERP at volume, Surfer SEO is the sharper tool and the safer pick.

But if your flow is research and draft from scratch on a budget, Frase does more of the work in one place for a fraction of the price. Decide by what you do all day: optimize → Surfer, research and write → Frase.
